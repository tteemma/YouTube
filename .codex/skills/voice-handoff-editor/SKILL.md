# Voice Handoff Editor

Require the script and clip review to be approved. Read `03-script.md` and shot
timings, then write `11-voice-handoff.md`.

Preserve the exact approved wording. Add target duration, section timing,
pronunciation and stress for names, number-reading guidance, short and long
pauses, emphasis, and emotional direction. Keep the detective voice calm,
curious, intelligent, and specific rather than sensational.

Flag timing conflicts instead of deleting facts or rewriting prose. Do not call
ElevenLabs, clone a voice, or create audio.
