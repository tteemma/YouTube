# Episode Topic Scout

Produce `00-brief.md`. Read `series/editorial-policy.md` when present.

For each candidate, define a central question and score 0-2 on surprise, human
stakes, causal clarity, visual potential, source potential, and satisfying
payoff. Recommend candidates scoring at least 9/12. Prefer one incident,
decision, object, myth, or turning point over a whole biography or era.

The approved brief must state: starting audience belief, ending understanding,
central question, stakes, likely visual anchors, scope exclusions, and why this
episode is materially different from recent episodes. Do not research detailed
claims or write narration.
