# Maltipoo Detective Character Bible

## Source images

- `data/Maltipoo Turnaround Sheet.png` — anatomy,
  proportions, costume, and camera angles.
- `data/Maltipoo Expression Sheet _ Retake.png` — facial
  expressions.
- `data/Maltipoo _ More Pixelated 1.png` — final pixel-art
  texture, palette, and lighting direction.

## Identity invariants

- Anthropomorphic brown Maltipoo detective with compact body and oversized head.
- White marking surrounds the character's anatomical left eye and continues
  down the anatomical left cheek.
- Anatomical left eye is blue; anatomical right eye is brown.
- Black fedora, brown three-piece suit, white shirt, black tie, black shoes.
- Floppy ears, rounded muzzle, black nose, warm brown curly fur.
- Detailed cinematic pixel-art illustration; never photorealistic.

## Role

The Maltipoo is a historical detective and narrator. He opens cases, examines
evidence, points to maps, observes reconstructions, and closes with a finding.
He is not silently recast as a historical person unless an episode explicitly
and intentionally uses a metaphorical cutaway.

## Default motion limits

Prefer blinking, a small head turn, one-paw pointing, opening one file, placing
one photograph, or a slow walk. Avoid lip sync, acrobatics, crowds touching the
host, or several consecutive hand actions in one generated clip.

## Reject when

- eye colors or facial marking are mirrored;
- hat, suit, body proportions, species, or pixel-art treatment drift;
- limbs merge, multiply, disappear, or change length;
- the character becomes photorealistic, plush, 3D, or human-faced.
