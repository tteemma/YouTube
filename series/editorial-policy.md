# Editorial policy

## Series promise

The Maltipoo detective investigates one surprising historical question and
shows the causal chain that answers it. The recurring idea is: history leaves
clues.

## Episode shape

- One incident, decision, object, myth, or turning point.
- One central question and one satisfying answer.
- Hook, promise, context, pressure, turn, result, meaning.
- Initial narration target: 145-175 Russian words and 60-75 seconds.
- A recurring host is allowed; interchangeable stories are not.

## Tone

Curious, intelligent, vivid, restrained, and accessible. Avoid generic
clickbait, fake certainty, present-day moralizing without context, shock for its
own sake, and imitation of another channel's catchphrases.

## Historical language

Distinguish documented fact, probable interpretation, disputed interpretation,
legend, and unknown. Never convert uncertainty into certainty for pacing.
