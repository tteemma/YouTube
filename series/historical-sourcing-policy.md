# Historical sourcing policy

Prefer primary sources, archives, museums, universities, peer-reviewed work,
and established reference institutions. Use secondary summaries for discovery,
not as the sole support for material claims.

For each source record author or institution, title, date, URL, source type,
relevant passage or locator, and known perspective or limitation. Material
causal, numerical, or contested claims require independent corroboration when
available.

Do not treat an AI answer, unsourced transcript, social post, SEO page, or another
Short as historical evidence. They may reveal a topic but cannot approve a
claim. Preserve disagreement and hindsight limitations.
